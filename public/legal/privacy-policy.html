Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

Welcome to ArtistToPlaylist's Privacy Policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when using our service, created by Julio Corcini. ArtistToPlaylist is an application that allows users to create Spotify playlists based on manually selected artists or your favorite artists.

2. Data We Collect

When using ArtistToPlaylist, we collect the following data from your Spotify account:

  • Display name
  • Spotify user ID
  • Email address
  • Country
  • Spotify profile URL
  • Profile image (if available)
  • Information about playlists created through ArtistToPlaylist
  • Information about your favorite artists (when you use the feature to create playlists from favorite artists)

This data is necessary for the basic functioning of the service and to create playlists in your Spotify account.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your Spotify account
  • Creating playlists in your Spotify account as requested
  • Displaying relevant information about your profile in the application interface
  • Maintaining a record of playlists you've created using our service
  • Improving the user experience and application functionality

We do not use your data to create marketing profiles or for any purpose not directly related to the functioning of ArtistToPlaylist.

4. Storage and Security

We store your data only for as long as necessary for the functioning of the application. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Spotify access tokens are temporarily stored in your browser (localStorage) and are used only to perform operations requested by you.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except:

  • With Spotify, for necessary application functionality (creating playlists, searching for artists, etc.)
  • When required by law or court order

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to access the data we have about you
  • Right to correct any inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to disconnect your Spotify account from ArtistToPlaylist at any time
  • Right to obtain a copy of your data in electronic format

7. How to Disconnect Your Account

You can disconnect your Spotify account from ArtistToPlaylist at any time using the "Disconnect Account" option available in the user interface. When you disconnect your account:

  • Your access tokens are immediately removed from your browser
  • You will need to authorize the application again if you want to use it in the future

To request complete deletion of your data stored on our servers, please contact us using the email indicated below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any significant changes through a notice in the application or by email (if available).
